# Code of Conduct

We want Hypermaker to be a useful, curious, low-ego place to build.

Participants must be respectful, assume good faith, discuss ideas rather than people, and keep criticism concrete and actionable. Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, threats, sexualized conduct, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not welcome.

Report a problem privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Include enough context to investigate. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and constructive.
